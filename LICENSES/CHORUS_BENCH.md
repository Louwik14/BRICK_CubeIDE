# Temporary chorus benchmark attributions

`CHORUS_DAISY` is an adaptation of the chorus engine in DaisySP by
Electro-Smith, Inc., MIT license. Source: https://github.com/electro-smith/DaisySP

`CHORUS_JUNO` is an adaptation of the single-mode DSP path in Junologue Chorus
by Peter All, MIT license. Source: https://github.com/peterall/junologue-chorus

The prototype keeps only the DSP ideas required by the stereo FX Track bench;
the original plugin/platform wrappers and build systems are not included.
